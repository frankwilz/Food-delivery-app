## Part 2: Team Agreement

### 1. Team Values

- **Respect:** We listen fully before responding and treat every idea as worth considering, whatever a person's experience level.
- **Accountability:** We do what we said we would do by the deadline we agreed to, or we say early that we can't.
- **Communication:** We share problems as soon as we notice them, not on the day something is due.
- **Reliability:** Teammates can count on us to show up, prepared, to meetings and lab.
- **Fairness and inclusion:** Work is divided evenly, and everyone gets a real chance to speak and to take on meaningful tasks.

### 2. Meetings

**How often**

We will meet twice a week:
- A 15 to 20 minute in-person check-in right after lab on Fridays (12:00pm) to review progress and assign tasks.
- A 30-minute online meeting on Tuesday evenings at 7:00pm (Discord) to check progress against deadlines.

**When and where**

The Friday check-in is at the library. The Tuesday meeting is online.

**If someone cannot attend**
- Tell the group chat at least 3 hours before, or as soon as possible in an emergency.
- The team posts meeting notes (decisions, tasks, deadlines) in the group chat within 24 hours.
- The absent member reads the notes and confirms their tasks within 24 hours of the meeting.

### 3. Communication

- **Everyday tool:** A group chat on Discord for questions, updates, and scheduling.
- **Urgent matters:** A text message or phone call to the teammate(s) involved, followed by a note in the group chat. "Urgent" means something due within 24 hours or something blocking the team's work.
- **Sharing information with the whole team:**
  - All work is kept in one shared GitHub repo with a shared task list showing who owns what and the due dates.
  - Decisions and meeting notes are posted in the group chat and pinned.
  - Do not approve your own pull request.
  - Nothing important is shared only in a direct message. If it affects the team, it goes to the group.

### 4. Response Expectations

- **Regular messages:** Respond within 24 hours.
- **Urgent messages:** Respond within 4 hours between 8:00am and 10:00pm.
- **If you cannot respond:** Post in the group chat as early as possible saying when you will be unavailable and when you will be back (for example, "Offline Thursday and Friday for a family trip, back Saturday morning"). If possible, arrange for a teammate to cover any deadline that falls in that window.

**If a member repeatedly fails to respond**
1. After 48 hours of silence, a teammate messages them directly (and calls for urgent matters).
2. After a second missed response, the issue is raised at the next meeting and the member's tasks are reviewed.
3. If it continues, the team emails the TA and instructor with a record of the attempts to reach the member.

### 5. Respect and Professional Conduct

**Respectful behavior means**
- Listening without interrupting.
- Criticizing ideas and work, not people.
- Giving feedback that is specific and constructive.
- Arriving on time and being present (phones away) during meetings.
- Crediting people for their contributions.

**Unacceptable behavior**
- Insults, sarcasm aimed at a person, or dismissive remarks.
- Ignoring messages or repeatedly showing up unprepared.
- Taking over someone's assigned work without asking.
- Submitting work that isn't the team's own or that the team didn't review.
- Discriminatory or harassing comments of any kind.

**Making sure everyone participates**
- At each meeting we go around the group so everyone speaks on each agenda item.
- Meeting roles (facilitator, note-taker, timekeeper) rotate weekly.
- Quieter members are asked directly for input, and no one is talked over.

### 6. Accountability

If a member misses a deadline or an agreed expectation, we follow these steps:

1. **Check in privately (within 24 hours):** A teammate messages the member to ask what happened and whether they need help or more time.
2. **Agree on a fix:** The member proposes a new deadline (usually within 24 to 48 hours) that does not put the team's due date at risk. Another member may help, but the original owner still finishes the work.
3. **Raise it at the next meeting:** If it happens a second time, the team discusses it openly and may reassign tasks or reduce the load and rebalance it.
4. **Involve the instructor or TA:** If it happens a third time or the team's grade is at risk, we email the TA with a summary of what was agreed and what happened.

### 7. Decision-Making

- **Method:** We aim for consensus, meaning everyone can support the decision, even if it was not their first choice. Every member gets a chance to share their view before we decide.
- **If we cannot reach consensus:** After 10 minutes of discussion we hold a majority vote, with each member having one vote. If the vote is tied, we pick the option that best matches the assignment requirements or ask the TA to clarify.
- **When members disagree:** Each person explains their reasoning, we compare the options against the assignment criteria and our deadline, and we vote if needed. Once a decision is made, everyone commits to it. The decision is recorded in the meeting notes so we do not reopen it without new information.
- **Small decisions:** Individual owners can decide details within their own task without a vote, as long as they tell the team.

### 8. Conflict Resolution

We resolve problems ourselves before involving the instructor or TA, using these steps:

1. **Talk directly and early:** The people involved discuss the issue privately within 48 hours, using "I" statements and sticking to specific behavior and its effect on the team ("The draft wasn't ready Thursday, and it delayed our review").
2. **Bring it to the team:** If it isn't resolved, we raise it at the next meeting with someone acting as a neutral facilitator (not one of the people in the conflict) and look for a solution that works for the team.
3. **Write down what we agreed:** The agreed solution and any deadlines go into the meeting notes.
4. **Involve the instructor or TA:** Only if steps 1 to 3 fail or the issue involves disrespect, harassment, or serious safety concerns, we contact the TA or instructor, sharing what we tried.

### 9. Anticipated Challenges

| Challenge | How we will address it |
|---|---|
| Missed meetings | Notify the team at least 3 hours ahead; read the notes and confirm tasks within 24 hours. If someone misses two meetings, we discuss changing the meeting time. |
| Missed deadlines | Set internal deadlines 24 hours before the real due date; report any risk as soon as it appears so tasks can be shared. |
| Unequal contributions | Track tasks in the shared list and review it each Friday; rebalance work if someone is carrying more than their share. |
| Poor communication | Use the group chat for anything affecting the team; follow the response times in Section 4. |
| Scheduling conflicts | Share exam and assignment schedules at the first meeting; use a shared calendar; be flexible about moving the Tuesday meeting as long as everyone can attend. |
| Disagreements | Follow the decision-making (Section 7) and conflict resolution (Section 8) steps. |
