## Hero generation prompt

Use case: ads-marketing. Asset type: photographic background for a food delivery website homepage. Create one original ultrawide panoramic hero background, approximately 2400 by 1000 pixels. Top-down professional food photography on a solid vivid warm vermilion orange-red surface, color around #f34427. An appetizing abundance of food crops into the outer edges: at the far left a beautiful ceramic noodle bowl with vegetables and chopsticks, upper left a little glass of iced citrus drink, lower right a white plate with colorful tacos and fresh herbs, far upper right a paper tray of golden fries. Make the center 60 percent of the composition generously empty red space, free of objects, so a large white headline and search field can later be placed there in HTML. Center empty space must remain completely clean at all heights except outer edge accents. Food looks real, fresh, detailed and naturally lit, with soft dimensional shadows. Premium studio editorial photography, crisp texture, warm appetizing color. The image is background artwork only, NOT a screenshot or mockup. No text, lettering, logos, brand names, watermarks, interface elements, phones, coupons or offers. Opaque background.

Each cell is a separate premium realistic food photograph, complete centered dish visible with generous small breathing room so it can be cropped for a card. Each dish is photographed from above or gentle three-quarter overhead, soft natural studio lighting, rich appetizing texture, pale warm neutral tabletop surfaces, clean stylish tableware, sharp food detail. Keep every subject fully contained in its own square cell, never cross cell boundaries. No text, no labels, no numbers, no logos, no watermarks, no people, no UI or screenshots.
